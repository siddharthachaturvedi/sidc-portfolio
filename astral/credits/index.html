DELTA T
Historical Delta T spline coefficients: L. V. Morrison, F. R. Stephenson, C. Y. Hohenkerk and
M. Zawilski, Addendum 2020 to Measurement of the Earth's rotation: 720 BC to AD 2015, Proceedings
of the Royal Society A 477:20200776 (2021), https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2020.0776. Numerical dataset
version 2 (https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.13885863.v2), licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Converted to program
literals without changing any epoch bounds or coefficients.
Modern monthly Delta T values: U.S. Naval Observatory, IERS Rapid Service/Prediction Center
(https://maia.usno.navy.mil/products/deltaT), approved for public release, distribution unlimited.
