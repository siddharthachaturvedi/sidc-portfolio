Accurate

To the arcsecond.

Every position comes from NASA JPL's DE440 ephemeris. The engine is checked against JPL, the IAU, and 1,381 eclipses in NASA's catalogues.

Private

By design.

Your chart is calculated and kept on your device. No account, no servers, no tracking.
