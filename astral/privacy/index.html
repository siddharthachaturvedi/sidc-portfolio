Privacy policy

Your chart stays on your device.

Last updated 27 September 2026

Astral Journey does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

  • Birth details, charts and your journal are stored only on your device, in a file that is encrypted whenever the device is locked. There is no account and no server.
  • Apple Health. If you connect it, the app reads and writes State of Mind (mood) entries for your own profile only. Health data never leaves your device, is never used for advertising, and you can revoke access at any time in the Health app.
  • Place search uses Apple Maps when you're online. Apple receives only the text you type into the search field, under Apple's privacy policy. Offline, the app searches a list bundled with it.
  • Your location, if you allow it, times sunrise, Rahu Kaal and the planetary hours for where you are. The app asks for an approximate location, matches it to a town in its bundled list, and keeps it on your device. You can choose a place by hand instead, or turn location off in Settings.
  • Ask the sky runs on Apple Intelligence on your device. Your questions are not sent anywhere.
  • Siri and Shortcuts answers are calculated on your device. A shortcut sends them to another app only if you build it to.
  • Sharing a prompt with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity or anyone else happens only when you choose to, through the system share sheet or the clipboard.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs; its one library, Nirayana, computes planetary positions and is open source.

Deleting a person removes their chart and journal. Deleting the app removes everything. You can export all your data from Settings at any time.

This website sets no cookies and collects nothing.
