      Ten years of releasing mosquitoes on purpose.

      On 18 October 2016, Singapore’s National Environment Agency released about 3,000 male mosquitoes carrying Wolbachia at Braddell Heights. This doodle follows the decade that turned that first release into a national programme, using only published numbers.
